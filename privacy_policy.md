# Privacy Policy for TripGuard

_Last Updated: September 23, 2025_

TripGuard (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard information when you use the TripGuard mobile application (the “App”).

---

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect the following categories of information:
- **Account Information:** Full name, phone number, email address, profile photo, and chosen role (driver or rider).
- **Location Data:** GPS coordinates to enable caution alerts, trip safety checks, and reporting features.
- **Media & Reports:** Photos, audio, documents, and other files you upload as evidence.
- **Device & Usage Data:** Device identifiers, app activity, crash logs, and diagnostics.
- **Communications:** Messages, comments, and reports shared within TripGuard forums or chats.

---

## 2. How We Use Information
We use your information to:
- Provide and improve TripGuard’s features (driver safety, rider cautions, forums, chat).
- Verify identity and protect against fraudulent or duplicate ride requests.
- Send alerts, push notifications, and updates about your account or community reports.
- Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and safety obligations.

---

## 3. Sharing of Information
We may share your information with:
- **Service Providers:** Vendors such as Firebase, Google Cloud, and other technology providers that power the App.
- **Community Members:** Information you post in public forums or caution reports may be visible to other users.
- **Legal Authorities:** If required by law, regulation, or safety investigations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

---

## 4. Data Security
- We use encryption, Firebase Authentication, Firestore security rules, and access controls to safeguard data.
- While we take reasonable measures, no system is 100% secure. Users are advised to exercise caution when sharing sensitive data.

---

## 5. Data Retention
- Account information is retained as long as your account is active.
- Reports and evidence may be stored for safety and compliance purposes.
- You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7).

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy
TripGuard is not intended for use by persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

---

## 7. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:
- Access, update, or delete your information.
- Opt out of push notifications or marketing communications.
- Request a copy of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at **tripguardapp@gmail.com**.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via in-app notifications or email.
